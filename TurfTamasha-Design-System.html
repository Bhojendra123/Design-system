The formula

Every thumbnail reads in the same order, so regulars recognise TurfTamasha before they read a word.

Status

LIVE in red, plus the slot badge: orange for mornings, cyan for nights. Highlights get a yellow tag.

Hook

A headline of three words or fewer, last line in yellow, with a four-word Hinglish subline in a yellow sticker band.

Matchup

Two team blocks in jersey colours, a split-screen rivalry, or one giant number with a score strip.

Colour

Built from the ground itself: turf greens, stump golds, ink and chalk. Red only ever means “streaming now”. Contrast shown on each chip is for the text colour drawn on it.

Spacing, corners & depth

An 8px base. Groups sit 40px apart; nothing comes closer than 48px to an edge.

Depth

  • Hard shadow 8px 8px 0 ink under team blocks and the score strip. No soft blurs.
  • Cut-out outline 8px chalk around every player or object cut-out.
  • Stump glow 32px yellow halo on one hero element only.
  • Text outline 4px ink when a headline sits on a photo instead of the panel.

Images

Every background comes from Unsplash, using only photos marked “Free to use under the Unsplash License”. Unsplash+ photos are skipped.

  • Default photo Turf Texture by Jason Dent
  • Size Landscape, at least 1920px wide; export 1280×720 JPG under 2 MB.
  • Blue-turf venues Green grass is recoloured blue automatically.
  • Credit Optional; suggested line for the description: “Background photo: name on Unsplash”.
  • Subject Off-centre, expressive faces and big moments beat empty pitches.
  • Cut-outs Background removed, 8px chalk outline, one glowing hero only.

Workflow

From match details to upload in six steps.

  1. Collect the detailsVideo type, venue, date, time and both teams; scores for highlights.
  2. Choose the templateLive, versus, highlights or last over.
  3. Write the hookHeadline of three words, subline of four.
  4. Get the Unsplash photoFree licence, landscape, 1920px or wider.
  5. Make three variantsChange the hook or the photo for B and C.
  6. Check and uploadShrink to 200px wide: hook and teams readable, bottom-right empty, matches the title. Then upload with Test & Compare.
